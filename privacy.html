Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Elvian is a learning assistant built by a student. This page explains in plain language what stays on your device, what is sent to other services, and the choices you have.

01Overview

Elvian (the website, the installed app and the optional backend) is made by Master Tonmoy Kumar Deb. There are no accounts to create, no advertising and no analytics or tracking scripts in Elvian. Most of what you do stays in your own browser.

This policy is written for a student project and is not legal advice. If Elvian is ever offered commercially, it should be reviewed by a qualified professional.

02What stays on your device

Elvian stores the following in your browser's local storage. It never leaves your device unless a section below says so.

  • Your chats, saved messages, saved answers and timers.
  • Your settings: theme, voice, background (including an image you upload), the AI address, model and API key you type, and the backend address.
  • If you sign in with Google: your first name and profile photo link.

Files and photos you attach are read on your device; only the text or the downsized picture needed for your question is sent to the AI service (see next section). You can delete chats in Settings → Delete all chats or clear the site data in your browser.

04Camera, microphone, notifications and clipboard

Elvian asks your browser for these only when you use a feature that needs them: taking a photo (camera), voice typing (microphone), timer and reply alerts (notifications) and Paste / Copy image (clipboard). You can withdraw each permission at any time in your browser's site settings.

05How data is protected

  • All text shown on screen is escaped, and a Content-Security-Policy limits which sites the page may load code from.
  • Files are read on your device and code you run executes in an isolated background worker.
  • API keys are never sent over an unprotected connection to a non-local address.
  • The optional backend checks the website origin, validates every request, limits wrong secret-code attempts and stores the lock code only as a salted hash.

No system is perfectly secure. Keep your device and browser updated, and protect your accounts with two-factor sign-in.

06Children

Elvian is intended for learners aged 13 and over. If you are under 18, please use it with a parent or guardian's guidance.

07Your choices

  • Delete your chats, saved messages and saved answers from Settings, or clear the site data in your browser.
  • Sign out from the sidebar to remove your stored name and photo link.
  • Change or remove the AI address, API key and backend address in Settings.
  • Turn off notifications in Settings and revoke permissions in your browser.

08How long data is kept

Data on your device stays until you delete it. The backend keeps prompt counters until it is reset or restarted. Other services keep data under their own policies.

09Changes to this policy

If Elvian changes how it handles data, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change.
