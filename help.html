01Getting started

  1. Open e-ELVIAN and type a question in the message box. Press Enter to send.
  2. Use the + button to attach a photo, file or camera picture.
  3. Every reload opens a fresh chat. Your earlier chats stay in the left sidebar.

03Files and pictures

Elvian reads pictures, PDF, Word, PowerPoint, Excel, OpenDocument, RTF, ZIP, code and text files, and shows details for audio, video and any other file. Attach with +, paste an image, or drag a file into the window. Reading happens on your device.

04Voice, timers and notifications

  • The speaker button reads an answer aloud and highlights each word. Choose the voice in Settings.
  • The microphone (Alt+M) stops after 3 seconds of silence and puts the words in the message box.
  • Timers ring with sound. Turn on Device notifications in Settings to be alerted while you are in another app.

05Install on phone or PC

Android (Chrome): ⋮ menu → Install app. iPhone / iPad (Safari): Share → Add to Home Screen. Windows / Mac: install icon in the address bar. After installing, press and hold the icon for shortcuts.

07Right-click menu

Right-click (or press and hold on a phone) anywhere in Elvian. The menu adapts to what you point at: copy, cut, paste, select all in the message box; copy paragraph, point, list or table in answers; copy or save a graph, picture or QR code; plus set a timer, lock / unlock the chat, new chat, Saved messages and Settings.

08Troubleshooting

"The free AI is busy"

The free service limits how many people can use it at once. Elvian retries by itself. For more speed, add your own key or endpoint in Settings.

Google sign-in says "no registered origin" (Error 401: invalid_client)

Add your site address (for example https://yourname.github.io, with no path and no slash at the end) under Authorised JavaScript origins for your OAuth client in Google Cloud Console, save, and try again after a few minutes.

I cannot hear an Indian English voice

On Windows open Settings → Time & language → Speech → Add voices → English (India) and choose Ravi or Prabhat. Microsoft Edge has the best voices.

A chat is locked

After 50 prompts a chat locks. Press Alt+K and enter the secret code held by the developer.

The logo or icon looks old

Refresh with Ctrl+F5. If you installed the app earlier, remove it and install it again.
